About me

A student who treats design and code as one craft.

I study UI/UX design, software engineering, mathematics and information security at UICT. Each subject sharpens the others: design tells me what to build, engineering makes it real, mathematics keeps my thinking precise, and security reminds me to protect the people who use it.

I like interfaces that are calm, readable and quick to understand, and I build them one screen at a time, finishing and checking each before moving on.

How I work

I start with who the product is for and what they need to do. Then I sketch low-fidelity layouts, refine them into high-fidelity screens, and build them as working apps.

I keep brand and detail consistent: a clear palette, one or two typefaces, and the same names for the same actions throughout.
